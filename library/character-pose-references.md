# Pose references for drawn characters

Use these tools when a person draws a character for a scene and needs a pose to work from. The [claim ledger](design-reference-claims.md) holds the evidence; the terms were read on 2026-11-01.

| Tool | Use for | Use boundary for a rendered film |
|---|---|---|
| [POSEMANIACS](https://www.posemaniacs.com/) | Posing a 3D figure to draw from | A drawing made from a pose may appear, including in a commercial film. The terms bar downloading the models or pose data, publishing screenshots of them, and accessing the site with automated tools, so a person uses it, not an agent. |
| [JustSketchMe](https://justsketch.me/) | Posing character models joint by joint | The licence allows personal and commercial material without credit. It does not say whether an unaltered model render may be shown, so mark such a render **unconfirmed**. Link justsketch.me; justsketchme.com redirects to an unrelated site. |
| [Line of Action](https://line-of-action.com/) | Gesture and figure practice from reference photos | Most photos are on loan for use inside the tool, so a drawing for a film you sell needs the photographer’s permission. The FAQ lets you post drawings you don’t sell. The site forbids scraping and AI training on its photos and drawings, so agents don’t fetch from it. |

In the run’s `assets-manifest.md`, list each drawn character with the tool it was drawn from and that tool’s boundary.
