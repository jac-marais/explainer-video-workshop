# Pose references for drawn characters

Use these tools when a person draws a character for a scene and needs a pose to work from. The [claim ledger](design-reference-claims.md) holds the evidence; the terms were read on 2026-11-01.

| Tool | Use for | Use boundary for a rendered film |
|---|---|---|
| [POSEMANIACS](https://www.posemaniacs.com/) | Posing a 3D figure to draw from | A drawing made from a pose may appear, including in a commercial film. The terms bar downloading the models or pose data and publishing screenshots of them. Article 12(1)(4) also bars accessing the service with automated tools at all. That is wider than a scraping ban: it covers an agent driving a browser, so a person operates the site. |
| [JustSketchMe](https://justsketch.me/) | Posing character models joint by joint | The licence allows personal and commercial material without credit. It does not say whether an unaltered model render may be shown, so mark such a render **unconfirmed**. Link justsketch.me; justsketchme.com redirects to an unrelated site. |
| [Line of Action](https://line-of-action.com/) | Gesture and figure practice from reference photos | Most photos are on loan for use inside the tool, so a drawing for a film you sell needs the photographer’s permission. The FAQ lets you post drawings you don’t sell. The site forbids scraping its photos and drawings and training AI on them. That bars bulk collection, not reading: an agent may browse it like a visitor, but must not download, store or collect its images. |

In the run’s `assets-manifest.md`, list each drawn character with the tool it was drawn from and that tool’s boundary.
