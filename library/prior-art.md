## Installation boundary

No upstream video skill or renderer was installed during research. HyperFrames' published `init` and `skills update` operations may refresh global skill sets. Inspect their target/current options before invoking them so a vendor installer does not silently overwrite or relocate skills. Use complete upstream bundles when selected; do not copy just an entrypoint and leave its scripts/references missing. Keep a tested package version pinned during a production run and re-check affected output when upgrading.
